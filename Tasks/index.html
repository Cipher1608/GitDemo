About Us

Laundry Mart is your neighborhood destination for clean, fresh, and hassle-free laundry. We offer a wide range of services, from self-service washers and dryers to convenient drop-off wash-and-fold options, so you can choose whatever fits your schedule. Our machines are modern, well-maintained, and available in multiple sizes to handle everything from daily wear to bulky comforters. The space is clean, safe, and comfortable, with free Wi-Fi and seating so you can relax or get some work done while you wait. For those short on time, our friendly staff can take care of everything, including dry cleaning, ironing, and pickup and delivery. At Laundry Mart, we're committed to making laundry day simple, affordable, and stress-free.
